# Limitations

- The contract is tailored to a German–Chinese learner lexicon, not a universal ontology.
- Validation cannot establish linguistic correctness.
- Simple mode has no independent AI review; full mode still is not human verification.
- IPA may be omitted with `—` when unreliable.
- `expected_pos` must be represented in a smaller local class vocabulary and may require a
  composite class.
- Provider token usage may be unavailable and is never estimated as exact.
- The public fixtures are synthetic demonstrations, not benchmark evidence.
